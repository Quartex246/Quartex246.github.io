Stop Saying "So Good"

2026-05-01
|
  • Thoughts
hits
This article contains 322 words.

A couple of days ago I posted a demo to a QQ group. It wasn’t finished yet, but the structure was basically there. Someone liked it, and I was happy. It meant I had finally stopped writing meaningless nonsense and had made something that at least resembled a real track.

But then almost everyone kept repeating the same thing. I forget the exact wording, but it boiled down to “So good?!”

It got annoying.

It’s not that I dislike the meme. I send Zimin stickers every day, and I yell “so good” at other people too. But seeing it repeated in perfect unison made my head spin.

The rhythm-game community seems to have developed this “so good” habit recently. No matter what you do, someone will reply with “so good.” The immediate impression it gives me is that everyone is insecure, and everyone is flattering everyone else.

Humility is a good thing. It forces us to re-examine ourselves, see the gap between us and others, and improve. But excessive humility is just self-doubt. Apart from making you beat yourself down, it seems completely useless.

I admit that praising others is good. But when everyone is praising you, praise loses all meaning. You should have a clear idea of your own level. If you’re good, be confident. If you’re not, push yourself to improve. With endless praise, some people lose themselves and believe they really are that good, while others suffer from the lack of objective evaluation and have no idea where to improve.

I also listen to demos in the group. Some of them aren’t particularly impressive, but someone will always spam “so good.” It feels more than a little fake.

Say less “so good” and more useful things. At least I’ll start with myself: don’t casually post “so good” anymore.
